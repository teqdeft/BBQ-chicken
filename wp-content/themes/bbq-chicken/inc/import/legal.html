2. What data do we process and why?

We process the personal data you provide via the franchise enquiry form and in related communications to respond to your expression of interest, contact you regarding franchise opportunities and manage your request.

Where you enquire about entering into a franchise relationship in your own name, the legal basis is the adoption of pre-contractual measures at your request and, if a contract is concluded, the performance of the contractual agreement (Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR). Where you act as the contact person or representative of a company interested in a franchise, the legal basis is our legitimate interest in maintaining professional contact and responding to that company’s enquiry (Article 6(1)(f) of the GDPR and Article 19 of the LOPDGDD).

If you do not provide the information marked as mandatory, we may be unable to contact you or deal with your request.

Any personal data provided for any of the purposes set out above must be true, accurate and complete, and must be kept up to date. Providing incorrect data will make it impossible for us to process your requests correctly.

We will not use your personal data for automated decision-making or profiling, nor will we process your data beyond what is described above.

3. What is the source of your data?

The data used to handle franchise enquiries come directly from you through the franchise interest form and the related communications.

4. To whom do we disclose your data?

As part of our data processing activities, we may disclose data to the following categories of recipients:

  • Delivery partners, so that customers can place their orders.
  • Banks and payment service providers, for the management of collections and other related payments.
  • Public authorities, regulatory bodies and competent authorities, where required by applicable legislation or in compliance with a legal obligation.
  • Judges and courts, where necessary to defend the organisation’s legitimate interests or in compliance with a court order.

5. How long will we retain your data?

We will retain your personal data for one year. Once this period has elapsed, the data will be duly blocked for as long as necessary to comply with legal obligations or for the establishment, exercise and defence of legal claims. Once the blocking periods have expired, the data will be permanently deleted using appropriate security measures to ensure its complete destruction, or it will be irreversibly anonymised.

6. How do we safeguard your data?

We take our responsibility to protect your personal data very seriously. To this end, we have adopted the necessary security measures, in accordance with the provisions of the GDPR and the LOPDGDD, to prevent, as far as possible and taking into account the digital context in which personal data is processed, the loss, misuse, alteration, unauthorised access or theft of data subjects’ data.

7. How can you control the management of your data?

Data subjects may, at any time and free of charge, exercise their rights of access, rectification, erasure and data portability, as well as the right to restrict processing and to object to processing, the right to withdraw consent (if relevant) and the right not to be subject to decisions based solely on the automated processing of their data, by sending their request by post to our registered office. In the event of an objection, we will cease processing the data, except where there are compelling legitimate grounds or for the exercise or defence of potential claims.

Data subjects are informed that if they consider that the processing of their data does not comply with current legislation, they may also lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency via its website: www.agpd.es.
