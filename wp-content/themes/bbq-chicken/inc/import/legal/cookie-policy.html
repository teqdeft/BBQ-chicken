We would like to inform you that at BLT GLOBAL BRANDS EUROPE, S.L. (“BLT Global Brands”) we use cookies to facilitate the use and navigation of the website https://www.bltglobalbrands.com/ (“Website”) and to improve its quality, in accordance with users’ browsing habits and styles.

1. What are cookies?

Cookies are files that are downloaded to the user’s computer, smartphone or tablet when they access the Website, and they allow us to store or retrieve certain information on a range of variables, such as the number of times the Website has been visited by the user, and to maintain the user’s session whilst they are browsing the Website.

Other similar technologies, such as tracking pixels, web beacons, device IDs or tracking URLs, also enable the collection of data on users, pages visited, links clicked, adverts displayed or emails opened.

It should be noted that blocking all cookies may disable certain services and features on the Website.

2. Why do we use cookies?

Cookies are useful for several reasons. They enable the Website to operate smoothly and in a way that is tailored to your preferences. They help us to remember, for example, that you are logged in or your preferences as a user of the Website. Thanks to the statistical information we collect via cookies, we can better understand users’ needs.

3. Types of cookies

Below, we provide information on the types of cookies used on the Website and their purposes.

Depending on which organisation manages the server or domain from which the cookies are sent and processes the data obtained, we can distinguish between:

  • First-party cookies: These are sent to the user’s device from the website.
  • Third-party cookies: These are sent to the user’s terminal device from a computer or domain that is not managed by the website operator, but by another entity that processes the data obtained through the cookies.

Depending on how long they remain active on the terminal:

  • Session cookies: These are a type of cookie designed to collect and store data whilst the user is accessing a website. As their name suggests, these cookies are stored on your device until the user’s browsing session ends.
  • Persistent cookies: These are a type of cookie in which the data remains stored on the device and can be accessed and processed for a period defined by the party responsible for the cookie, which may range from a few minutes to several years.

By purpose:

  • Technical cookies: these are cookies that enable the user to navigate the website or platform and use the various options or services available on it, such as controlling traffic and data communication, identifying the session, accessing restricted-access areas, using security features whilst browsing, or storing content for the streaming of videos or audio, or sharing content via social media. These include session and persistent cookies.
  • Personalisation cookies: these are cookies that allow the user to access the service with certain predefined characteristics based on a series of criteria on the user’s device, such as the type of browser used to access the service, the regional settings from which the service is accessed, etc.
  • Analytical cookies: these enable us to quantify the number of users and thus carry out statistical measurement and analysis of how users utilise our website. To this end, we analyse your browsing behaviour on our website with a view to improving it.
  • Advertising cookies: these enable us to analyse users’ browsing habits on the internet so that we can display adverts related to those habits.

On this website, we use technical cookies necessary for the site to function and third-party analytical cookies (Google Analytics), which allow us to obtain statistical information on the use of the website. For further information: https://policies.google.com/ and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Analytical cookies will only be installed on your device if you give your consent via the cookie banner or settings panel displayed when you access the website. If you do not give your consent, these cookies will not be installed and the essential functionality of the website will not be affected.

4. Who uses cookies?

The information collected via the Website’s cookies may be used both by BLT Global Brands and by a third party providing a service to the website owner. In this regard, the information from the cookies used on the Website is processed by BLT Global Brands and by third parties, such as Google, in relation to user behaviour.

5. Managing Cookies

Taking into account the information provided in this Cookie Policy, below we set out how you can manage the cookies used on the Website using the various options offered by the most common browsers (Chrome, Firefox and Edge). When you visit our website for the first time, we will display a cookie banner or settings panel with an explanation of cookies. As a user, you can access your browser’s preferences menu and configure it so that no cookies are installed, with the exception of those strictly necessary for the Website to function, delete existing cookies and/or clear them before browsing other pages on the Website. If you prevent all cookies from being installed on the Website, some of its features, as well as certain content, may be affected.

6. How can you view, disable or delete cookies?

All browsers allow users to search for and view the cookies installed by a particular website, obtain information regarding their duration, and delete them. Furthermore, to disable, restrict, block or delete cookies, users must adjust the settings of the browser they are using on their device, whether it be a computer, smartphone or tablet. Below are links to information on the main browsers:

  • Google Chrome: https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647?hl=es
  • Mozilla Firefox: https://support.mozilla.org/es/kb/habilitar-y-deshabilitar-Cookies-sitios-web-rastrear-preferencias
  • Microsoft Edge: https://support.microsoft.com/es-es/microsoft-edge/eliminar-las-Cookies-en-microsoft-edge-63947406-40ac-c3b8-57b9-2a946a29ae09
  • Set your browser to reject all cookies, thereby ensuring that no cookies from any website are installed on your device.
  • The browser will notify you before a cookie is installed so that you can decide whether or not to accept it.
  • The browser should only block third-party cookies from the websites you visit, but not those used by the website you are currently browsing.
  • The private browsing option, whereby cookies are stored on your device but are automatically deleted when you finish browsing the website: Google Chrome: https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95464?hl=es, Mozilla Firefox: https://www.firefox.com/es-ES/, and Microsoft Edge: https://support.microsoft.com/es-es/microsoft-edge/explorar-inprivate-en-microsoft-edge-cd2c9a48-0bc4-b98e-5e46-ac40c84e27e2.
