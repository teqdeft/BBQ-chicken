Our story

Bringing communities together through delicious food

Founded in 1995, bb.q Chicken (Best of the Best Quality) was born with a clear vision: to share the authentic taste of Korean fried chicken with the world.

Managed across Europe by BLT Global Brands, we combine traditional recipes, proprietary crispiness techniques, and world-class operational standards. With over 3,500 locations across 57 countries, we believe that great food has the power to connect people.

We are more than a restaurant brand. We are a global culinary experience dedicated to quality, innovation, and bringing communities together through delicious food.

The food

What makes it Korean fried chicken

Korean fried chicken is fried twice. The first fry cooks it, the second makes it shatter. Then comes the sauce.

Double-fried

Fried twice, so the coating stays crisp even under a glaze.

Sauced, not smothered

Secret Sauce, Honey Garlic, Galbi, Jasoman or Gangjeong, brushed on right after frying.

Made for sharing

Buckets, boxes and sides meant for the middle of the table.

Chicken University

Where every bb.q cook learns the recipe

Since 1999, bb.q has trained owners, managers and kitchen teams at its own campus in Korea. It's also where the R&D team develops new flavours. European partners and their teams train on the same programme.
