Spain is bb.q's door to Europe

8 December 2025 · Europe · 2 min read

bb.q is coming to Europe. On 8 December 2025, Genesis BBQ Group announced that it has set up bb.q's European headquarters in Spain, through a joint venture with BLT F&B Group.

A launch in Seoul

Officials from both companies marked the start with a launch ceremony at Genesis BBQ's headquarters in Seoul. According to The Korea Herald, BLT F&B Group runs food and beverage brands in Spain and the United States, including Domino's Pizza, Applebee's and IHOP.

Why Europe

Genesis BBQ sees Europe as the last big step in taking its Korean fried chicken worldwide. The region is one of the world's largest tourism markets, with customers who have strong purchasing power and a taste for premium food. According to the company, brands with a real story behind their culture and quality do well there.

What the Spanish headquarters will do

The headquarters in Spain is the hub for bb.q's European supply chain: local production, sourcing of ingredients, regional logistics and distribution. That speeds up the rest: adapting the menu to local tastes, opening flagship restaurants and signing franchise partners.

Where bb.q opens first

The first flagship restaurants are planned in Spain, the United Kingdom, France, Poland and the Netherlands. From there, bb.q will grow into neighbouring countries step by step.

Genesis BBQ Group chair Yoon Hong-geun called Europe "a strategic gateway to complete K-food's globalization". He said the brand will bring the premium value of Korean chicken across Europe, with localised menus and an advanced logistics network.

Sources: The Korea Herald and The Korea Times, both 8 December 2025. Also reported in Spanish by Yonhap.
