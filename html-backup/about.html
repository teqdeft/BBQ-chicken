BLT Global Brands

Global excellence, local expertise

BLT Global Brands acts as a strategic master partner and operating force across European markets. We bridge the gap between world-class brand heritage and neighbourhood-level execution through agile infrastructure, robust supply chain logistics, and dedicated local marketing support.

Our core commitment is to bring the most beloved brands to neighbourhoods worldwide while maintaining high ethical standards and improving the quality of life for our communities, customers, and employees.

Great Korean fried chicken, on every high street in Europe.

We want bb.q to be the chicken Europeans pick on a Friday night, for a family dinner or a quick lunch. We'll get there with strong local partners, restaurants that run well and food people come back for.
